BEFORE YOU PAY ANYONE

Four truths,
free of charge.

Some things you should know for free, whether you hire us or not. We start there.

  • Government fees are public. The official fee schedule of Migraciones is published on its website and updated every year. Anyone who won't show it to you next to their own fees is charging you for opacity. We always itemize both, line by line.
  • Apostilles can only be done by you — nobody can do them on your behalf. They are issued in your home country, often online and same-day. We tell you exactly which ones you need, free of charge; paying for remote "apostille management" is usually paying for nothing.
  • Your Paraguayan residency does not erase your home-country taxes by itself. Migration residency and tax residency are two different things: the second is built, and your exit from your home country's system is planned with a tax adviser there. Anyone promising "zero taxes" on the strength of a card alone is selling you a future problem.
  • The USD 5,000 bank deposit no longer exists. Migration Law 6984/2022 abolished it: no deposit in a Paraguayan bank is required to obtain residency today. Solvency is evidenced through other means — we tell you which one applies to your case. Anyone still asking for it is working with the rules of years past.

So what do we charge for? What genuinely earns a fee: choosing the right route, filing a correct case the first time, and answering for it to the end. That — in writing.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What everyone asks,
answered straight.

How long does residency take?

Processing times are set by the public agencies and vary by route and nationality — which is why we don't publish timelines that don't depend on us. What you do get: an estimated timeline for your case, in writing, in the initial assessment, and notice at every step of the file.

Which documents do I need?

A valid passport, birth certificate, criminal record certificate covering the last 5 years, and civil status certificate where applicable — all apostilled in your country. We confirm the exact list for your case in the first consultation, free of charge.

How many times do I have to travel?

The filing before Migraciones is done in person, so at least one trip is required. We coordinate the agenda so a single short trip is enough: local clearances, filing and biometrics on consecutive days. Afterwards, if you have already flown home, we follow the file under a power of attorney.

How much does it cost in total?

Every case is quoted privately: write to us on WhatsApp and you receive a single written quote before you commit, with professional fees and government charges itemized separately and explained. No surprise costs.

When do I receive the cédula?

It is processed at Identificaciones once residency is granted: we book the appointment, accompany you on the day, and coordinate the delivery.

Can you guarantee approval?

The final decision rests with Migraciones and with each bank; no one can seriously guarantee it. What we do control: that your file enters complete and correct — which is what prevents rejections and delays in practice.

Do I stop paying taxes in my home country?

Not automatically — and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. Migration residency and tax residency are two different things: Paraguay does not tax your foreign-source income, but your home country only stops taxing you if you exit its system properly, and that is planned with a tax adviser there. We build the Paraguayan side and tell you honestly which part does not depend on Paraguay.

Can I do the process myself, without a lawyer?

Yes — the law does not require you to hire anyone, and some steps only you can do (your apostilles, your criminal records). What you hire us for is something else: the right choice of route, a file that enters correctly the first time, someone who answers at every office, and follow-up to the end. We tell you from the start what you can do yourself and what is worth leaving to us.
