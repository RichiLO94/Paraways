V

V · AND RESIDENCY · INVESTOR PASS · RES. MIC 0283/2026

A home is a purchase.
Residency is a case file.

Buying a lot for your own home is an ordinary property purchase. Residency by investment has rules of its own — amount, purpose, evidence — and we assess it with you case by case, free of charge and in writing.

Permanent from day one10-year cédulaFamily included1 day every 3 years

The rule

  • The Investor Pass is direct permanent residency by investment, with no temporary stage: a 10-year cédula, spouse and minor children included under a single investment, one day of presence every three years to keep the status.
  • Real-estate category: from USD 200,000, for rental, appreciation or productive use. Exclusively personal use is excluded.
  • The investment must have been made, or be demonstrably under way.

Who decides, and what we do

Eligibility and granting are decided by SUACE and Migraciones. Our job is making sure your file arrives impeccable: we tell you in writing which part of a purchase at Le Petit Trianon may count and which may not, before you buy anything.

The other categories — productive from USD 70,000, tourism from USD 150,000, financial instruments from USD 200,000 — are explained on the main page.

WHAT PEOPLE USUALLY ASK

Can I buy a lot as a foreign national?

Yes. Foreign nationals may acquire real estate in Paraguay. The purchase is formalised by public deed before a notary and registered with the Public Registry. The definitive measurements are those of the title.

Where is the pricing?

In private, on WhatsApp. This page tells you how much each lot measures, not how much it costs: that way every conversation starts with the same information on the table. You write, name the lot if you have already chosen one, and receive the full fact sheet of the development in writing.

What is surveyed and what is drawn in the model?

The site boundary is that of the georeferenced survey (GNSS RTK, UTM 21S, 2026-06-05). The internal layout — street, pavements, lots, gate, green area and bamboo belt — follows the architect’s subdivision plan, and the lot areas were calculated on the survey. The show home follows the architect’s render and its facades are illustrative. The tree cover you switch between Park and Woodland is scenery. The technical note (in Spanish) explains it step by step.

Does buying a lot give me residency?

Buying a lot for your own home is an ordinary property purchase and does not count, on its own, towards the Investor Pass: the real-estate category requires an investment from USD 200,000 for rental, appreciation or productive use, already made or demonstrably under way. Eligibility and granting are decided by SUACE and Migraciones. If your plans include residing in Paraguay, we assess it with you case by case, free of charge and in writing.

How far is it from the lake? How long from Asunción?

San Bernardino lies some 50 km east of Asunción, on the shore of Lake Ypacaraí. We go through the distance from the site to the lake, and the travel times, with you, map in hand, on WhatsApp. Here are the coordinates of the site centre — 25°19′59.5″ S · 57°15′24.2″ W — to measure it on the map of your choice.

What happens after I write?

You receive the full fact sheet of the development, with the pricing, privately and in writing. If the Investor Pass interests you, we assess your case with the rule in front of us. If you decide to buy, the purchase is formalised by public deed before a notary and registered with the Public Registry. Every step is discussed with you before it is taken.

Can I see the model on my phone and in my language?

Yes. On a phone it opens full screen: drag to rotate and tap a lot to open its details. It is available in Spanish, Portuguese, English and French, and asks for no personal data. The technical note (in Spanish) explains how it was built.
