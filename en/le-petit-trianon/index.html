VI · QUESTIONS

Can I buy as a foreign national?

Yes. Foreign nationals may acquire real estate in Paraguay. The purchase is formalised by public deed and registered with the Public Registry.

Does the plot give me permanent residency?

The plot alone falls below the minimum investment. Together with the construction of your house, an investment of USD 200,000 or more, held for rental or appreciation, can be submitted under the Investor Pass: direct permanent residency and a 10-year cédula. Exclusively personal use does not qualify. The decision rests with SUACE and Migraciones; we assess your case in writing before you buy, free of charge.

What does the price include?

The investment — plot and house — and every formality: Investor Pass, permanent residency, cédula, RUC and banking support, for one overall price, itemised and in writing before we start. Anything not included (documents and apostilles from your country, travel) is also set out in writing from day one.

How long does it take?

A few months from purchase, as an estimate: official timeframes are set by SUACE, Migraciones and Identificaciones. You receive a written timeline and an update at every stage.

Do I have to travel to Paraguay?

Yes: biometrics for the cédula are taken in person. We plan your schedule so you make the most of your stay; everything else is handled remotely.

Do I have to live in Paraguay?

No. You keep permanent residency by spending one day in Paraguay every three years.
